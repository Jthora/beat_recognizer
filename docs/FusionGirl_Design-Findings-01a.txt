The Harmonic/Musical layer specifically — "two different twelves," proven distinct
This is the most numerically precise section and directly answers your question about zodiac↔pitch mapping.

Key correction on the wiki (superseding an earlier draft): musical pitch's twelve and the icosahedral zodiac's twelve are different groups, not the same structure:

Zodiac twelve = V₄ × C₃ (Klein four-group × cyclic-3: element × modality), realized on the icosahedron.
Musical twelve = C₁₂ (cyclic group of order 12: circle of fifths / stacked ratios), realized on a dodecagon, sitting on a prime-ratio lattice (primes 2,3,5…), not the icosahedron.
Proof cited: "the icosahedral group A₅ has no element of order 12, so a 12-cycle cannot be realized on the icosahedron." V₄ ≠ C₄ (Klein four has no element of order 4).
Both twelves arise from the same arithmetic fact though: 12 = 2² × 3 — the Circle of Fifths closes because twelve ascending fifths ≈ seven octaves; the zodiac's twelve comes from 4 elements × 3 modalities = (2×2)×3.

This layer is about human speech articulation, not music per se, but is the "vocal mapping" layer the USGS hub references.
